## Adaptation Map

| Maintained Rule | Original Grounding | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `h1` reader-context | Readability rule: audience frame; voice profile: explain without talking down. | Supply relevant missing context while respecting expertise and avoiding repetitive background. |
| `h2` answer-then-explain | Readability rule: main point first and progressive disclosure. | Make the answer apparent and explain its support; no mandatory opening sentence or response sequence. |
| `h3` one-idea-at-a-time | Readability rule: one idea per paragraph and coherent sentence-length guidance; voice profile: examples carry explanations. | Make relationships between ideas understandable without imposing sentence, paragraph, or example patterns. |
| `h4` meaning-before-mechanics | Readability rule: common words and technical detail follows prose; voice profile: meaningful technical references. | Explain technical significance with familiar language; leave reference placement, voice, and vocabulary choices to judgment. |
| `h5` preserve-precision | Readability rule: Fidelity wins and the reader's requested shape. | Preserve material facts and qualifications; allow requested summarization without importing a ledger, full rubric, or extra verification work. |

The latency comparison retains the source rule's 340 ms and three-of-ten-window example. The other comparisons are illustrative cases authored for this adaptation, not upstream quotations or observations of this repository. Their wording and arrangement are not templates.

The selection excludes a separate prose-structure principle, fixed lengths, heading/list policies, mandatory detail placement, a personal writing persona, vocabulary bans, and the editor/self-check workflow. The complete originals remain available to explain the source and adaptation without activating those omitted instructions. The full repository license notice accompanies the maintained catalog so offline publication retains attribution.
